The Exposition

You are Shay, a business entrepreneur who's only ever business was a spell shop that flopped so hard you never paid off the debt you took on to start it.

Fortunately for you though, you still have the sick grimoire that your Grandad passed down to you before he passed away, and it is that grimoire which ow interests the tenants of The Lions Estate.

You have been invited to the mansion to use your grimoire to solve an unspecified problem that the tenants were having. Based on the image above, it would sound like a terrible idea, however there is an upside! The owner of said mansion is filthy rich, and will likely pay off your debts if you solve this problem for him.

With stars (dollars) in your eyes, you set off for the mansion, to earn your reward!
