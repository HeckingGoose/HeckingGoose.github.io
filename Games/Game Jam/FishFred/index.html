What is it?

Fish Fred is a short game about helping a flying fish (Fish) make his way over to the nearest vet to fix his sudden inability to fly.

You take on the role of some random guy with a fish bowl collecting habit (Fred), who has decided to help this fish out on their journey.

But what about the gameplay?

The game stars a whopping two puzzles based around throwing a fish very safely (no fish were harmed in the making of this game), along with insightful dialogue between Fish and Fred.

The context?

This game was a submission to the 2026 March Three Thing Game Jam, the three things for this game were:

  • Flying Fish,
  • Emergency,
  • Crying.
