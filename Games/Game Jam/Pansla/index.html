PanSla

What is it?

Take to the fight against the cursed spirits in a state of the art (you cannot prove otherwise) mech suit, equipped with dual rocket launchers that only occasionally self-detonate on firing.

Due to minor design flaws with the mech, firing missiles and repairing put quite the toll on the poor thing's engine. Fire too many missiles or repair for too long and you'll quickly have yourself an engine fire, so keep your extinguisher handy unless you wanna be cremated!

With all those boring minor details out the way, let's get to the real interesting stuff! War is an adventure, a game even! And in this game you can fight an ever-increasing horde of cursed spirits to gain an ever-increasing score! After all, everyone wants to see how big the number can get, so go ahead and make a big number for us :D
